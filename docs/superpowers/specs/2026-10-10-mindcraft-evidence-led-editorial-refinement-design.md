# MindCraft Evidence-Led Editorial Refinement Design

## Decision

This refinement keeps MindCraft's approved digital identity and the executive-first information architecture, but replaces the repeated dark-SaaS presentation with an evidence-led editorial system. It treats supplied imagery as sparse contextual evidence, keeps the copy short at the point of decision, and lets specialist routes retain the depth a qualified visitor needs.

The visual objective is not to make the existing supplied AI-style illustrations look more synthetic through glow, collage or repetition. It is to make them less dominant: typography, tonal surfaces, rules and deliberate whitespace carry the experience; imagery is framed, cropped and used once for a specific contextual purpose.

## Evidence and Constraints

- The audit covered all 19 addressable routes at desktop and representative mobile/tablet widths.
- The current Overview exposes approximately 835 visible words; Life Sciences exposes approximately 851; About exposes approximately 963. The depth is valuable, but too much of it competes for first-pass attention.
- Current visual repetition comes from pervasive `#050505` canvas use, rounded dark bordered modules, indigo-glow CTAs, panoramic AI-style scenes and broadly repeated fade-up movement.
- The approved brand system explicitly permits obsidian `#050505`, card slate `#0A0A0A`, active blue-black `#0D0F17`, deep-indigo `#1E1B4B`, indigo `#6366F1`, emerald `#10B981` and amber `#F59E0B`.
- All existing `NavTab` routes, five primary journeys, data explorers, deep links, enquiry flow, keyboard behaviour and responsive image utility remain in scope and functional.
- No remote stock imagery, generated imagery, generated portraits, invented clients, metrics, case studies, certifications, partners or outcomes may be introduced.
- The verified MindCraft enquiry destination remains `info@mindcraftconsultancy.com`; Dayanand Poojari continues to use the original supplied portrait wherever his identity is represented.

## Chosen Visual World: Obsidian Fieldbook

MindCraft should feel like a considered executive fieldbook: precise, calm and materially structured. It is not a black-background AI product, a neon intelligence dashboard or a conventional consultancy brochure.

### Surface rhythm

The approved palette gains hierarchy through purposeful sequencing rather than new brand colours.

| Surface role | Approved colour | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inkwell | `#050505` | Header, data-heavy explorers, page edges and quiet pauses. |
| Graphite | `#0A0A0A` | Reading surfaces, ruled lists and restrained content sections. |
| Blue-black | `#0D0F17` | Active choice, tool state and compact contextual callouts. |
| Deep indigo | `#1E1B4B` | One high-authority statement surface per primary journey; never a generic background. |
| Semantic accents | Indigo, emerald and amber | Navigation, state, category and one restrained optical highlight—not ambient glow. |

The overview and route families must alternate these surfaces. A page may begin in deep indigo or graphite; it may not begin and end as an uninterrupted sequence of black rounded panels. Full-opacity indigo CTA glow, radial "AI" halos and gradient text are removed from the new direction.

### Typography and structure

Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono remain the only type system. Outfit uses fewer, stronger statements; Plus Jakarta Sans carries short readable explanation; JetBrains Mono is reserved for coordinates, labels and data.

- A route opening has one H1, one supporting paragraph and no more than two actions.
- A primary content moment has one assertion, a short proof or explanation, then a next action or disclosure—not a paragraph, list, list caption and second CTA.
- Display copy targets 10–18 words; supporting copy targets 25–55 words.
- Section headers have more space above than below and no decorative eyebrow above the headline.
- Cards remain only for choices, comparisons, data controls and responsive disclosure. Editorial copy moves to ruled lists, grids, offset columns and full-width bands.

### Evidence frames

"Hyper frames" are adopted as **evidence frames**: an image is anchored within an editorial composition rather than being a generic banner or card background.

- Every primary asset family has one deliberate route assignment and one crop treatment.
- An evidence frame may be full bleed, inset, offset, portrait-like or edge-cropped, but no image becomes a second background on another route.
- Image captions explain the contextual role only when needed; they do not invent a case study or outcome.
- Art is never covered by a large glass panel, overlaid with excessive tags, or reused as a hero and final CTA scene.

## Content Architecture

### Overview

The Overview becomes five moments, targeting 420–520 visible words before footer content:

1. A typography-led proposition on a deep-indigo or graphite surface. The current global montage leaves the first viewport; it remains available for a single market-context use later in the route.
2. Four capability choices: concise question, answer and destination. Their detailed service lists remain in their pillar routes.
3. One combined proof composition for Life Sciences depth, African market fluency and responsible AI. It links outward rather than restating the full industry, region and governance inventories.
4. A compressed engagement view: three readable phases with the six existing steps retained in expandable or linked detail.
5. A focused enquiry close with one distinct office/strategy evidence frame, not another synthetic hero.

The homepage no longer independently carries the full Life Sciences sector list, the whole wider-industry list, all regions, several insight cards and six long process descriptions.

### Capabilities

Business Consulting, Market Research, AI Training and AI Enterprise Solutions retain their own routes. Each route displays 250–350 initial visible words, then places sourced detail in the existing selected state, disclosure or contextual tool.

The page grammar is consistent but not cloned:

- Business Consulting: decision sequence and boardroom evidence frame.
- Market Research: evidence flow and research-mapping frame.
- AI Training: learning pathway and workshop frame, with amber signal.
- AI Enterprise Solutions: operational pattern and operations frame, with emerald signal.

No route repeats the generic capability definition, a large service card grid and a second enquiry panel in one scroll.

### Markets, Life Sciences and About

The interactive Market and Life Sciences routes retain data density because visitors come to answer a specific question. Their introductions reduce to a concise purpose statement and orienting action; their filters, selectors, route links and source-grounded information remain untouched.

- Life Sciences keeps all six documented subsectors exactly: Pharmaceuticals, Medical Devices, Medical Diagnostics, Medical Equipment, Surgical Consumables, and Hospitals & Healthcare Providers. Its initial visible copy targets 500–600 words, with sector evidence accessed by selection.
- About targets 450–550 initial visible words, preserving MindCraft's purpose, working principles, senior expertise route, verified portrait and enquiry distinction.
- Insights remains a dedicated editorial index. Overview carries at most one selected thinking link, not a multi-card feed.
- The Engagement Builder remains optional and fully functional. It is introduced as a tool for visitors who already know their direction, never as the required route to enquiry.

## Motion and Interaction Direction

The implementation weights three lenses differently:

1. **Emil Kowalski, primary:** movement must orient or confirm; interactions are fast, interruptible and generally under 250ms. Keyboard-triggered changes do not animate.
2. **Jakub Krehel, secondary:** menus, disclosures and modal state changes use subtle opacity, short translation and small blur transitions; exits are quieter than entries.
3. **Jhey Tompkins, selective:** one rare, large-screen evidence-frame reveal may use an experimental CSS treatment if it communicates context. No looping animation, bounce or decorative scroll theatre is introduced.

The global reduced-motion guard remains authoritative. The current pulsing application indicator is replaced by a static active treatment. Generic fade-up reveals will be removed from content that repeats across every route; retained motion is limited to navigation, modal/dialog orientation, one visual reveal per route family and meaningful interactive state changes.

## Implementation Boundaries

- Preserve React/Vite architecture, `NavTab`, `NAV_ITEMS`, public props and existing data sources.
- Extend `src/index.css` and `src/components/ui/primitives.tsx` before adding local surface classes so the system remains shared.
- Recompose `HeroOperatingSystem.tsx` and `LandingSections.tsx` first; then apply the new grammar to the capability, specialist and About route families in place.
- Preserve `Header.tsx`, `Footer.tsx`, `GlobalCommandSearchModal.tsx`, `EngagementIntakeModal.tsx` and `EngagementBuilder.tsx` functionality. Refine their surfaces/motion only where the shared system demands it.
- Do not delete a route or disable a working explorer to reduce visible content. Route consolidation occurs through hierarchy, disclosure and contextual entry points.
- Do not add a parallel navigation model, component library, image utility or design-token system.

## Validation Contract

Every changed route is inspected at 390px, 768px and 1440px for visual hierarchy, crop, contrast, readable text measure, overflow, fixed-height collision, focus visibility, direct hash entry, active navigation state and reduced-motion behaviour.

The final quality gate runs `npm run lint`, `npm run build`, the installed Impeccable detector on changed markup-bearing files and browser verification of primary enquiry/modal, menus, disclosures and deep-route returns. A successful build alone is never considered visual validation.

## Out of Scope

This refinement does not create new photo assets, claim unsupported client work, alter factual source material, convert the site to a router framework, change the verified enquiry destination, or remove the established executive-first navigation model.

## Approved Art-Direction Amendment — 2026-10-10

The earlier evidence-frame wording is refined by direct user feedback. Supplied art should feel immersive and naturally integrated with the obsidian canvas, rather than appearing as a collection of isolated rectangular, rounded or bordered windows.

- Restore the original Overview opening composition: `overview/global-*` is again the one full-bleed hero image behind “Turn complexity into meaningful business progress.” The deep-indigo type-only hero is removed.
- Remove isolated image boxes, visible card borders and rounded-corner framing from editorial art. Art may still be cropped for its subject, but it must extend to a page edge, a section boundary or the full width of its grid side.
- Use the existing `Scrim` utility for deliberate canvas-black (`#050505`) and charcoal (`#0A0A0A`) fade fields. The fade protects readable overlay text and lets art dissolve into the surrounding surface; it is not a generic tint.
- A short route assertion or factual caption may sit in the dark, quiet region of an image. It must have enough contrast, use no glass card, and never cover data controls or a subject’s face.
- Accent light is restrained: at most a small approved semantic hue in a fade edge or label. There are no purple hero backgrounds, coloured text gradients, neon bloom or wide CTA shadows.
- Each supplied image family remains assigned to its existing route. A route uses one primary image treatment; visual variety comes from crop, direction and placement rather than duplicating the art.
- Apply the same grammar to Overview, capability, Markets, Life Sciences and About routes. Data explorers remain tool-first; their information panels do not receive decorative photo overlays.

The revised design still targets reduced copy, clear navigation and source-grounded content. All existing verification requirements remain in force.
