# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are senior decision-makers at multinational, regional and growing organisations who need a credible partner for growth, market entry, operational change, market intelligence or practical AI adoption. Life Sciences and healthcare leaders evaluating African-market work are a priority specialist audience. Secondary audiences include public institutions, development partners, research organisations, academic institutions and innovation hubs.

## Product Purpose

MindCraft Consultancy Services helps organisations turn business complexity into practical progress through integrated Business Consulting, Market Research and Intelligence, AI Training and Digital Literacy, and AI Enterprise Solutions. The website should help a visitor recognise their challenge, understand the relevant capability and begin a conversation.

## Positioning

Nairobi-headquartered MindCraft combines African market understanding and international consulting standards with specialist Life Sciences expertise and four connected consulting, intelligence and applied-AI capabilities. Engagements can begin with one focused need or span multiple capabilities.

## Operating Context

MindCraft works across Africa as its primary focus market and also supports clients in Europe and the United States. Work may include market-entry and expansion strategy, primary and secondary research, organisational and process change, practical AI capability-building, and AI-enabled workflow or intelligence solutions. Delivery can be in-person, virtual or hybrid, from focused assignments and short courses to longer programmes and retainers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the four documented capabilities: Business Consulting; Market Research and Intelligence; AI Training and Digital Literacy; and AI Enterprise Solutions.
- Retain Life Sciences as MindCraft's founding heritage and specialist depth while communicating relevance across the documented wider economy.
- Keep African market knowledge, localisation and responsible AI grounded in the supplied source documents.
- Preserve existing routes, navigation targets, accessibility and working interactions; consolidate hierarchy and presentation rather than delete factual capability coverage.
- Do not invent clients, statistics, case studies, credentials, partnerships, offices, regulatory claims or quantified outcomes.

## Brand Commitments

MindCraft is a deliberate, credible consultancy rather than a generic AI product. Its approved digital system uses an obsidian dark interface, Outfit display type, Plus Jakarta Sans body type, JetBrains Mono metadata, restrained semantic accents, precise motion and factual copy. Existing brand marks and supplied imagery are the visual source material.

## Product Principles

1. Make the visitor's business question clearer before presenting a service catalogue.
2. Lead broadly for executives, then use Life Sciences and African market fluency as evidence of depth.
3. Keep complex capability information scannable, with depth available only when a visitor needs it.
4. Translate insight into a practical next step, not a vague promise.
5. Let clarity, editorial restraint and real source material create credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA-conscious contrast, visible keyboard focus, minimum touch targets, semantic hierarchy, meaningful image alternatives, reduced-motion support and responsive recomposition at mobile, tablet and desktop widths.
